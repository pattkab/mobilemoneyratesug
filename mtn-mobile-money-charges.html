How to read the tax columns

Tax min and tax max are the 0.5% withdrawal tax at the bottom and top of that band. If you withdraw UGX 100,000, you are in the UGX 60,001–125,000 band. The MTN fee is UGX 1,925. The tax is 0.5% of 100,000, which is UGX 500, inside the published UGX 300–625 range for that band. Use the calculator for the exact shilling.

Send money on MTN

The send column is the fee to an MTN number or another network. Receiving into a MoMo wallet is free. Depositing cash is free. See how to send with *165# and the wallet-to-bank fees.

Account limits on the MTN page

  • Minimum transaction: UGX 500
  • Maximum transaction: UGX 5,000,000
  • Minimum balance: UGX 0
  • Maximum balance: UGX 20,000,000
