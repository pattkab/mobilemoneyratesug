FAQ

How much does MTN charge to send mobile money in Uganda?

MTN’s published send fee is UGX 100 up to UGX 5,000, UGX 500 from UGX 5,001 to 60,000, UGX 1,000 from UGX 60,001 to 500,000, UGX 1,500 up to UGX 1,000,000, and UGX 2,000 up to the UGX 5,000,000 transaction limit. The same fee covers MTN and other networks.

How much does Airtel Money charge to send money in Uganda?

Airtel’s published send fee to another Airtel Money user is UGX 100 up to UGX 5,000, UGX 500 from UGX 5,001 to 60,000, and UGX 1,000 from UGX 60,001 up to UGX 7,000,000. Airtel’s own page says UGX 1,000 is the highest sending fee in that range.

How much is the MTN withdrawal charge?

MTN agent withdrawal starts at UGX 330 for UGX 500–2,500 and rises by band to UGX 20,000 for UGX 4,000,001–5,000,000. Add the 0.5% government withdrawal tax on the amount you cash out.

How much is the Airtel Money withdrawal charge?

Airtel agent withdrawal follows the same lower bands as MTN, then stays at UGX 12,500 from UGX 500,001 through UGX 7,000,000. The 0.5% withdrawal tax is added on top.

Is there a tax on sending mobile money in Uganda?

The 0.5% tax applies to cash withdrawals, not to sending money, receiving money, bill payments, or wallet-to-bank transfers.

What is free on MTN MoMo?

Deposits, receiving money, airtime, bundles, balance checks, mini statements, PIN changes, registration, and roaming access are free on MTN’s tariff page.
