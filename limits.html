Free on MTN

  • Depositing cash onto MoMo
  • Receiving money into a MoMo wallet
  • Buying airtime for yourself or another number
  • Buying voice, internet, and messaging bundles
  • Checking your balance
  • Mini statement on the MoMo app or *165*8#
  • Changing your MoMo PIN
  • Registering MoMo on your SIM
  • Using MoMo while roaming
  • Viewing a statement in the MoMo app

Free on Airtel

  • Receiving Airtel Money into an Airtel Money wallet
  • Receiving international transfers into Airtel Money
  • Registering for Airtel Money
  • Depositing cash at an agent (the sender or depositor is not charged a deposit fee)
